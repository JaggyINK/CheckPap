# Notes for App Review

Copy into App Store Connect > App Review Information > Notes.

---

Check Pap is an independent information app that helps foreign nationals in France (and French citizens moving abroad) prepare their administrative procedures. It is not affiliated with the French government, does not impersonate any administration, and states on every main screen: "Independent service. Government procedures are free of charge."

How it works:
- The user answers a short questionnaire; the app shows the documents that apply to their situation, the steps, where to submit, and key dates.
- Every piece of information shows its public source (service-public.gouv.fr, the ANEF website of the Ministry of the Interior, prefecture websites, Légifrance, OFII...) and the date it was checked. Links open in Safari.
- The app gives general information and guidance only: no legal advice, no promise of outcome, no submission on behalf of the user, no payment.

No account is needed. To test:
1. Launch the app, choose a language, enter any French postcode (e.g. 94000) or tap "Later".
2. Tab "Démarches" / "Procedures" > "Séjour" > "Renouveler mon titre de séjour", answer the questions, then see the checklist.
3. Tab "Mes droits" / "My rights": answer the profile questions to see the rights that may apply.
4. Tab "Actualités" / "News": recent changes published by the administration.

Privacy: no data is collected; everything stays on the device. Calendar uses the system event editor (no calendar access requested). Notifications are local and only scheduled when the user turns reminders on.
