## Description (4000)

Check Pap helps you prepare your administrative procedures in France, one step at a time.

Answer a few simple questions: the app gives you the list of documents that match your situation, with examples, the steps to follow and where to submit your application.

PROCEDURES
• Renew or change your residence permit
• Replace a lost or stolen permit, report a move
• Bring your family, travel with your child
• Apply for French citizenship (naturalisation, marriage)
• Passport, identity card, visa to come to France, travel outside France
• First tax return, rental guarantor (Visale), driving licence exchange
• Moving abroad: preparing to leave and guides for 200 countries

FOR EACH PROCEDURE
• A personalised checklist of documents
• "Why this document?" and "How to get it?" (e-photo, tax stamp, translation...)
• Steps, key dates and reminders
• Your prefecture, found with your postcode
• Letter templates when an online procedure is not possible

MY RIGHTS
A few questions, then the rights and benefits that may apply to you: health, housing, work, family, banking, appeals, with the organisations to contact.

NEWS
Changes to laws, fees and deadlines that affect foreign nationals, as published by the French administration.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
No account, no ads, no data sent anywhere.

INDEPENDENT SERVICE
Check Pap is not a government service. Government procedures are free of charge. The app informs and guides you based on public sources (service-public.gouv.fr, the ANEF website, prefectures...); it does not replace the information from your prefecture and does not give legal advice. Each piece of information shows its source and the date it was checked.
