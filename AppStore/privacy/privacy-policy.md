# Check Pap privacy policy

Last updated: [TO COMPLETE: publication date]

Check Pap is an independent service that helps people prepare administrative procedures. This page explains what information the app uses and where it stays.

## In short

- **No data is collected.** Nothing you enter is sent to Check Pap or anyone else.
- **No account, no ads, no tracking**, no analytics.
- **Everything stays on your phone**, and is deleted if you delete the app.

## What the app stores on your phone

Only to help you, and only on your device:

- your chosen language and your postcode (to find your prefecture);
- your answers to the questionnaires, the documents you ticked or removed, the steps you completed;
- the expiry date of your residence permit, if you enter it;
- the appointments you saved (date, place, notes);
- the details you type to prepare a letter (name, address, foreign national number...), so you do not have to type them again.

This information may be included in your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer), depending on your own settings. Check Pap has no access to it.

## What the app only does when you ask

- **Calendar**: "Add to calendar" opens the iPhone's own screen; you confirm it. The app does not read your calendar.
- **Notifications**: reminders are scheduled on your phone (no server), only if you turn them on.
- **Letters**: when you copy, share or email a letter, it goes through the app you choose (Mail, Messages...).
- **Links**: links to government websites open in your browser; those websites have their own privacy policies.

## Deleting your data

Delete a file in the "My files" tab, or delete the app: all saved information is erased.
